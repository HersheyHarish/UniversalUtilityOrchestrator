# Credits, Discounts, and Loyalty Programs

## Section 1.0: Loyalty Credit

Customers who have maintained an active account in good standing for
12 consecutive months or more are eligible for a monthly loyalty credit.
The loyalty credit is calculated as 2% of the base plan charge and is
applied automatically. For a customer on the Residential Saver 12 plan
with a $180.00 base charge, the loyalty credit would be $3.60 per month.

The loyalty credit appears on the bill as "Loyalty Credit" with a
negative amount (reducing the total).

## Section 2.0: Autopay Discount

Customers enrolled in automatic payment (autopay) receive a $5.00
discount per billing cycle. The discount appears as "Autopay Discount"
on the bill. To qualify, the customer must have a valid payment method
on file and must not have had a returned payment in the prior 6 months.

## Section 3.0: Paperless Billing Credit

Customers who opt into paperless billing (electronic statements only)
receive a $2.00 credit per billing cycle. This credit appears as
"Paperless Billing Credit" on the bill.

## Section 4.0: Referral Credit

Customers who refer a new customer receive a one-time $25.00 credit
applied to their next bill after the referred customer completes their
first billing cycle. The referred customer also receives a $25.00 credit.
There is no limit to the number of referral credits a customer can earn.

## Section 5.0: Solar Export Credit (Net Metering)

Customers with eligible solar installations who export excess generation
to the grid receive a credit at the net metering rate of $0.06 per kWh
exported. Solar export credits appear as "Solar Export Credit" on the
bill and are calculated based on the grid export meter readings.

Credits exceeding the monthly bill amount roll over to the next billing
cycle. Accumulated credits exceeding $500.00 may be requested as a
direct payment once per calendar year.

## Section 6.0: Demand Response Credit

Customers enrolled in the Peak Rewards demand response program receive
credits for reducing consumption during demand response events. The
credit rate is $2.00 per kWh reduced below the customer's baseline
during the event window. Credits are applied in the billing cycle
following the event.

## Section 7.0: Budget Billing

Customers may opt into budget billing, which averages the annual
estimated cost across 12 equal monthly payments. Budget billing
smooths seasonal variation but does not change the total annual cost.
A true-up adjustment is applied annually.
