# Seasonal Peak Surcharge Policy

## Section 1.0: Purpose

The seasonal peak surcharge helps offset increased generation and
transmission costs during periods of high electricity demand. The surcharge
is applied only during designated peak seasons and is calculated based on
the customer's consumption during peak hours within those seasons.

## Section 2.0: Peak Season Dates

The summer peak season runs from June 1 through September 30 each year.
The winter peak season runs from December 1 through February 28 (or 29
in leap years). Months outside these windows are considered off-peak and
no seasonal surcharge is applied.

## Section 3.0: Peak Hours

Within peak season months, peak hours are defined as:
- Weekdays: 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM local time
- Weekends and federal holidays: no peak hours apply

Consumption during peak hours is tracked via the smart meter at 15-minute
intervals and aggregated per billing cycle.

## Section 4.0: Surcharge Calculation

The seasonal peak surcharge is calculated as:

    peak_kWh = sum of consumption during peak hours in the billing period
    surcharge = peak_kWh × $0.08

The surcharge appears on the bill as "Summer Peak Surcharge" or
"Winter Peak Surcharge" depending on the season.

## Section 5.0: Surcharge Cap

The seasonal peak surcharge is capped at $75.00 per billing cycle.
If the calculated surcharge exceeds $75.00, only $75.00 is billed.

## Section 6.0: Peak Avoidance Tips

Customers can reduce their peak surcharge by shifting high-consumption
activities (laundry, dishwashing, EV charging) to off-peak hours.
Smart home integrations and scheduled appliance timers are recommended.
Customers enrolled in the Peak Rewards program may receive additional
credits for reducing usage during demand response events.

## Section 7.0: Exemptions

Customers on medical life-support equipment exemption plans are not
subject to the seasonal peak surcharge. Customers must provide
documentation from a licensed physician to qualify for this exemption.
