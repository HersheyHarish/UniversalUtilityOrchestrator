# Taxes, Fees, and Regulatory Charges

## Section 1.0: City Utility Tax

A city utility tax is applied to all residential electric service accounts.
The tax is calculated as a percentage of the total pre-tax bill amount
(base plan charge + overage + surcharges, before credits).

The current city utility tax rate is 5.5%. The tax appears on the bill
as "City Utility Tax" and is remitted directly to the local municipality.

## Section 2.0: State Regulatory Fee

A state regulatory fee of $3.50 per month is applied to all residential
accounts to fund the state public utility commission's oversight activities.
This fee is fixed and does not vary with usage.

## Section 3.0: Renewable Energy Fund Charge

A renewable energy fund charge of $0.002 per kWh is applied to all
consumption to support the state's renewable energy development goals.
This charge appears as "Renewable Energy Fund" on the bill.

## Section 4.0: Late Payment Fee

Bills not paid by the due date (25 days after the billing date) are
subject to a late payment fee of $15.00 or 1.5% of the outstanding
balance, whichever is greater. Late fees are waived once per 12-month
period for customers in good standing upon request.

## Section 5.0: Returned Payment Fee

Payments returned by the bank (insufficient funds, closed account, etc.)
incur a returned payment fee of $25.00 per occurrence.

## Section 6.0: Tax Exemptions

Customers who qualify for tax-exempt status (e.g., certain nonprofit
organizations, government entities) may apply for a city utility tax
exemption by submitting a valid tax-exempt certificate. Exemptions take
effect on the next billing cycle after approval.
