# Billing Disputes and Adjustments

## Section 1.0: Filing a Dispute

Customers may dispute any charge on their bill within 60 days of the
billing date. Disputes can be filed through:
- The online account portal (Account > Billing > Dispute a Charge)
- Customer support phone line (1-800-555-ENERGY, Mon-Fri 8 AM - 8 PM)
- Written correspondence to the billing department

When filing a dispute, the customer should reference the specific line
item, the billing period, and the reason for the dispute.

## Section 2.0: Investigation Process

Upon receiving a dispute, the billing team will:
1. Place the disputed amount on hold (not subject to late fees)
2. Review meter data for the billing period in question
3. Cross-reference with any known outages, meter malfunctions, or
   system errors
4. Provide a resolution within 5-10 business days

If the investigation requires a meter re-read or field inspection,
the resolution timeline may extend to 15 business days.

## Section 3.0: Resolution Outcomes

Dispute investigations result in one of the following:
- **Adjustment issued**: The charge is reduced or removed, and a credit
  is applied to the next billing cycle.
- **Charge confirmed**: The original charge stands. The customer is
  provided with supporting meter data and a detailed explanation.
- **Partial adjustment**: A portion of the charge is adjusted based on
  findings (e.g., partial meter malfunction).

## Section 4.0: Escalation

If the customer is not satisfied with the dispute resolution, they may
escalate to the billing supervisor within 30 days of the resolution
notice. Final escalation may be directed to the state public utility
commission.

## Section 5.0: Meter Accuracy Verification

Customers may request a meter accuracy test at no charge once per
12-month period. Additional tests within the same period incur a $50.00
testing fee. If the meter is found to be outside the acceptable accuracy
range (±2%), the testing fee is waived and the customer's bill is
retroactively adjusted for the estimated period of inaccuracy, up to
a maximum of 6 billing cycles.

## Section 6.0: Estimated Bills

If a meter reading cannot be obtained (meter access issues, communication
failure), the bill is estimated based on the customer's average
consumption over the prior 3 billing cycles. Estimated bills are clearly
marked. The next actual reading triggers a true-up adjustment that
corrects any over- or under-estimation.
