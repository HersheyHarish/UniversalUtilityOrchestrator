# Residential Saver 12 — Plan Terms & Conditions

## Section 1.0: Plan Overview

The Residential Saver 12 plan is a fixed-rate residential electricity plan
with a 12-month contract term. The plan provides a base monthly rate covering
a set amount of electricity usage (the "monthly baseline"), with overage
charges applied to consumption exceeding the baseline.

## Section 2.0: Monthly Baseline Allowance

Each billing cycle, the customer receives a baseline allowance of 1,000 kWh.
Usage within the baseline is covered by the base plan charge. The baseline
does not roll over between billing periods — unused baseline allowance in
one month does not increase the allowance in the following month.

## Section 3.0: Base Plan Charge

The base plan charge is $180.00 per month and covers the first 1,000 kWh
of consumption. This charge appears on the bill as "Base Plan" and is fixed
for the duration of the 12-month contract term regardless of actual usage.

## Section 4.0: Overage Rates

Usage exceeding the monthly baseline of 1,000 kWh is billed at the overage
rate of $0.14 per kWh. Overage charges are calculated as:

    overage_kWh = total_usage - 1000
    overage_charge = overage_kWh × $0.14

Overage charges appear on the bill as "Usage Overage" and are calculated
based on meter readings at 15-minute intervals aggregated over the billing
period.

## Section 5.0: Early Termination

Customers who cancel the Residential Saver 12 plan before the 12-month
contract term expires are subject to an early termination fee of $150.00.
The fee is waived if the customer switches to another plan of equal or
greater value.

## Section 6.0: Plan Renewal

At the end of the 12-month term, the plan automatically renews on a
month-to-month basis at the then-current rate unless the customer opts
out or switches plans at least 14 days before the renewal date.

## Section 7.0: Billing Disputes

Customers may dispute any line item on their bill within 60 days of the
billing date by contacting customer support or submitting a dispute through
the online account portal. Disputed amounts are placed on hold and are not
subject to late fees during the investigation period, which typically takes
5-10 business days.
