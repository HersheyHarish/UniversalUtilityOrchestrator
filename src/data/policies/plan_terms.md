# Residential Plan Terms & Conditions

This document covers the two residential rate plans offered: Residential Saver 12
and Standard Residential. Each plan defines its own baseline allowance, base
charge, and overage rate. Common policies (early termination, renewal, billing
disputes) appear at the end and apply to both plans.

## Section 1.0: Plan Overview — Residential Saver 12

The Residential Saver 12 plan is a fixed-rate residential electricity plan
with a 12-month contract term. The plan provides a base monthly rate covering
a set amount of electricity usage (the "monthly baseline"), with overage
charges applied to consumption exceeding the baseline.

## Section 2.0: Monthly Baseline Allowance — Residential Saver 12

Each billing cycle, the customer receives a baseline allowance of 1,000 kWh.
Usage within the baseline is covered by the base plan charge. The baseline
does not roll over between billing periods — unused baseline allowance in
one month does not increase the allowance in the following month.

## Section 3.0: Base Plan Charge — Residential Saver 12

The base plan charge is $180.00 per month and covers the first 1,000 kWh
of consumption. This charge appears on the bill as "Base Plan" and is fixed
for the duration of the 12-month contract term regardless of actual usage.

## Section 4.0: Overage Rates — Residential Saver 12

Usage exceeding the monthly baseline of 1,000 kWh is billed at the overage
rate of $0.14 per kWh. Overage charges are calculated as:

    overage_kWh = billable_usage - 1000
    overage_charge = overage_kWh × $0.14

Overage charges appear on the bill as "Usage Overage" and are calculated
based on meter readings at 15-minute intervals aggregated over the billing
period. For customers with on-site solar generation that is not enrolled in
the Solar Net Metering plan, billable usage equals grid import only — solar
energy consumed directly by the household is not metered or billed.

## Section 5.0: Early Termination — Residential Saver 12

Customers who cancel the Residential Saver 12 plan before the 12-month
contract term expires are subject to an early termination fee of $150.00.
The fee is waived if the customer switches to another plan of equal or
greater value.

## Section 6.0: Plan Renewal — Residential Saver 12

At the end of the 12-month term, the plan automatically renews on a
month-to-month basis at the then-current rate unless the customer opts
out or switches plans at least 14 days before the renewal date.

## Section 7.0: Billing Disputes

Customers may dispute any line item on their bill within 60 days of the
billing date by contacting customer support or submitting a dispute through
the online account portal. Disputed amounts are placed on hold and are not
subject to late fees during the investigation period, which typically takes
5-10 business days. This policy applies to all residential rate plans.

## Section 8.0: Plan Overview — Standard Residential

The Standard Residential plan is a flexible month-to-month rate plan with no
contract term and no early-termination fee. Compared to Residential Saver 12,
the plan has a much lower monthly base charge but a smaller baseline allowance,
which makes it well-suited to lower-usage households or customers with on-site
solar generation that materially reduces their grid import.

## Section 9.0: Monthly Baseline Allowance — Standard Residential

Each billing cycle, the customer receives a baseline allowance of 300 kWh.
Usage within the baseline is covered by the base plan charge. As with
Residential Saver 12, the baseline does not roll over between billing periods.

## Section 10.0: Base Plan Charge — Standard Residential

The base plan charge is $25.00 per month and covers the first 300 kWh of
billable usage. This charge appears on the bill as "Base Plan" and applies
each month regardless of actual usage.

## Section 11.0: Overage Rates — Standard Residential

Usage exceeding the monthly baseline of 300 kWh is billed at the overage
rate of $0.14 per kWh. Overage charges are calculated as:

    overage_kWh = billable_usage - 300
    overage_charge = overage_kWh × $0.14

Overage charges appear on the bill as "Usage Overage". As with Residential
Saver 12, billable usage for solar-equipped households not enrolled in
Solar Net Metering is grid import only.

## Section 12.0: Plan Term & Renewal — Standard Residential

The Standard Residential plan has no fixed contract term. Customers may
switch plans or cancel service at any time without incurring an early-
termination fee. The plan continues on a month-to-month basis until the
customer elects to switch or cancel.
